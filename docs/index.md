## Design and development history

The [product specification](product-specification.md), [stabilization inventory](stabilization.md)
and documents under [architecture](architecture/) preserve implementation decisions and
phase-specific evidence. Earlier claims and screenshots describe their recorded
revision; current user instructions and release qualification take precedence.
Historical notes are retained for traceability rather than presented as the repo's
getting-started flow. The future roadmap includes possible host-security/CVE analysis;
it is outside the version 1.0 feature scope.
